# RVM EG --- Creator Landing Page Blueprint (V1)

## Goal

Build a premium landing page that makes creators feel they are applying
to a real company, not filling out a Google Form.

### Primary CTA

**Apply as a Creator**

Screen → Admin Dashboard Review
